About this register

All figures are the real, published eSAKSHI/MPLADS "Allocated Limit for Hon'ble MPs" dataset — 543 Lok Sabha MPs and 232 Rajya Sabha MPs. This register scans that dataset directly for genuine data-quality issues (missing values, duplicates, invalid amounts) rather than simulating anomalies.
